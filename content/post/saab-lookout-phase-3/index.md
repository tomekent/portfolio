Mind Foundry has been selected by Saab UK to work together on Phase 3 of the LookOut programme, funded through the Defence and Security Accelerator (DASA) on behalf of the Royal Navy.

The project aims to mature and de-risk new technology that will inform the Royal Navy's future Maritime Early Warning capability, working alongside Saab UK and in close collaboration with DSTL. It's a good example of the kind of applied, defence-focused machine learning work I've been leading at Mind Foundry — taking early-stage research and pushing it towards real operational impact.

You can read Saab's full announcement here: [Saab UK awarded LookOut Phase 3 contract](https://www.saab.com/markets/united-kingdom/stories/saab-uk-awarded-lookout-phase-3-contract) (24 January 2024).
